Verdict

Path 1 — NOT achievable. The exact 4-month window to 31 Dec 2026 needs

30,379.88 BYN/month, which is **5.6× the actual 5,463 BYN/month disposable

surplus**. Saving 100% of the surplus for all 4 months accumulates only

21,852 BYN (≈7,193 USD) — about 18% of the 40,000 USD target. There is

no scenario within the confirmed constraints (cash-only, no financing,

8,000 BYN net income, 2,537 BYN expenses) under which this path closes by

the stated date.

Path 2 — Achievable, but only with the deadline moved from 31 Dec 2026

to ~late July 2028. Saving the full 5,463 BYN/month surplus at 0% return

reaches 120,186.00 BYN at 22 months (still 1,333.52 BYN short) and

125,649.00 BYN at 23 months (clears the 121,519.52 BYN target with 4,129.48

BYN to spare). 23 months is the minimum timeline that fully covers the

target, contingent on 100% of the surplus being preserved, uninterrupted,

for the entire period.

Path 3 — Achievable, and grounded in a real, dated Belarus-market

listing rather than an estimate. At 5,463 BYN/month: the low-end target

(77,969.96 BYN) clears at 15 months (81,945.00 BYN); the most-likely target

(98,099 BYN) clears at 18 months (98,334.00 BYN); the high-end target

(115,188.35 BYN) clears at 22 months (120,186.00 BYN). All figures were

independently recomputed and verified during quality review and matched

exactly. This path is faster than Path 2 across its entire range but

requires accepting a used car instead of the originally stated new car, and

residual price uncertainty (year/mileage/trim) means the ~18-month figure is

a well-supported estimate, not a guarantee, until confirmed with real

quotes.

Overall: the user's exact original goal (new Mazda CX-5, 40,000 USD, by

31 Dec 2026, cash-only) is not achievable. A path to the goal exists in

two forms — extend the timeline and keep the full target (Path 2), or

accept a used car for a materially faster, though still delayed, timeline

(Path 3) — so the honest characterization is achievable with changes

to either the deadline or the "new vs. used" requirement, not a flat "not

achievable."

Recommendation

**Recommended path: Path 2 — full target (new car), extended ~23-month

timeline.**

Path 2 is recommended as the primary path because it is the only one of the

three that preserves every one of the user's originally stated requirements

unchanged: the same car (new Mazda CX-5), the same budget (40,000 USD /

121,519.52 BYN), cash-only funding, no financing. The single variable that

has to change is the target date — from 31 Dec 2026 to ~late July 2028. Its

calculation is single-valued and fully verified (bracketed exactly between

22 and 23 months), with no price-estimation uncertainty, unlike Path 3's

price range.

Trade-off against Path 3 (used car, ~15–22 months): Path 3 gets the

user into a car meaningfully sooner — as much as 5–8 months earlier at the

most-likely price point, and even its high-sensitivity case ties Path 2's

22nd-month position while Path 2 needs a full 23rd month. Its grounding is

solid (a real, dated Belarus marketplace listing as the primary anchor, with

a bounded, cross-checked sensitivity band), but it requires giving up the

"new car" requirement and carries more residual price uncertainty (final

cost depends on the specific unit's year/mileage/trim) than Path 2's

single fixed BYN target.

**This is a genuine priority trade-off for the user to decide, not a

purely mathematical one:**

  • Choose Path 2 if keeping a new car matters more than arrival date.
  • Choose Path 3 if getting into any Mazda CX-5 sooner (5–8+

months earlier) matters more than it being new — but budget time to get

2–3 concrete quotes before committing to a specific date.

Path 1 (the original exact deadline) is not recommended under any

circumstance without changing at least one of: the deadline, the target

price, the no-financing assumption, or an increase in income — none of

which are currently in scope.
